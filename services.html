Google Business Profile Management

A complete, up-to-date profile is often the first thing a nearby customer sees. We keep yours accurate and active.

What it involves

We review and optimise your Google Business Profile so customers see correct, complete and current information when they find you on Search and Maps. Ongoing management keeps the listing active instead of leaving it as a one-time set-up.

Who can benefit

Clinics, restaurants and hotels, retail stores, CA and professional offices, solar installers and any business that serves customers in a specific area.

Key deliverables

  • Profile audit and optimisation (categories, description, services, attributes)
  • Business information and opening-hours updates
  • Regular post creation and publishing
  • Photo and logo management
  • Review-response guidance with reply drafts
  • Periodic performance monitoring using Google's own insights

Objectives it can support

  • More accurate and trustworthy local listings
  • Better local discovery on Search and Maps
  • More calls, direction requests and website visits
  • A responsive, credible public presence

Please note: Google decides how profiles are displayed. We do not promise specific ranking positions.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Sustainable visibility starts with a website that search engines can understand and people want to use.

What it involves

We study how your customers search, then improve your site's content, structure and technical health so it can be found for relevant queries. For location-based businesses we also strengthen local search signals.

Who can benefit

Businesses with a website that want steadier, relevant organic traffic: professional firms, schools and colleges, e-commerce stores, manufacturers and service providers.

Key deliverables

  • Keyword and search-intent research
  • On-page optimisation (titles, headings, content, internal links)
  • Technical SEO checks (speed, mobile usability, indexing, structure)
  • Local SEO recommendations
  • Content recommendations and topic ideas
  • Performance monitoring and reporting

Objectives it can support

  • Improve relevant organic visibility over time
  • Attract more qualified website visitors
  • Fix technical issues that hold a site back
  • Build content that answers customer questions

Please note: SEO takes time and results depend on competition and search-engine behaviour. We do not guarantee rankings.

Website Design & Management

Your website is your most important marketing asset. We design it well, then help you keep it healthy.

We design responsive, easy-to-navigate websites and landing pages with clear calls to action. After launch, we can continue to manage content, updates and improvements. Website design and website management are separate scopes.

Who can benefit

New businesses needing a first website, established businesses with an outdated site, and anyone running campaigns who needs focused landing pages.

  • User experience improvements
  • Basic on-page SEO set-up

Objectives it can support

  • A credible, professional online presence
  • Clear paths for visitors to enquire
  • A site that works well on mobile
  • A reliable base for SEO and advertising

Ongoing website management

A separate, recurring service for content updates, small improvements, monitoring and maintenance after launch. Quoted monthly or as agreed.
